# AmbatuFit information architecture

Figma source: [AmbatuFit](https://www.figma.com/design/mF4qd88hUkVYoUojfMWPSk/AmbatuFit?node-id=5-81)

Five tabs use exact IDs `Home`, `SymptomAssessment`, `Guidance`, `Progress`, and `Profile`. `Login` is the unauthenticated route. Detail routes are `AssessmentResult`, `AssessmentHistory`, `Medications`, `MedicationDetail`, `HealthProfileEdit`, `Nutrition`, `Gyms`, `Professionals`, `ProfessionalDetail`, `Booking`, `MyBookings`, and optional `ProfessionalSharedAssessment`. Internal ownership follows Personal Health Intelligence (including Medication Information & Safety Guidance), Consultation & Booking, and Identity & Access. Partner data remains owned by AmbatuNoutrish and AmbatuMove (`ambatumove`).
